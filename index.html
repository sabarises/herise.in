OUR BELIEF

Behind every product, every service, every order — there is a woman who showed up for another woman.

A home cook selling tiffin boxes to fund her daughter's school fees. A retired teacher tutoring a neighbour's son for an hour of web design in return. A talented designer crafting logos to build her independent career from home.

Herise isn't just a marketplace. It's a trust economy — where a woman's word, her verified profile, and her community are worth more than any algorithm. We're building a world where economic independence is accessible, safe, and communal.

✦ THE HERISE PLATFORM

Services & Opportunities

For Providers: A comprehensive ecosystem designed to let every woman monetize her unique skills from home. Whether you're teaching, creating, or connecting, there's a space for you here.

For Buyers & Clients: A trusted, premium marketplace where you can discover authentic, high-quality services, handcrafted products, and expert consultations directly from verified women.

Computer & Digital Services

Work on a laptop or smartphone for clients locally or globally.

  • Design & Branding: Logos, business cards, social media graphics, UI/UX designs.
  • Social Media & Ads: Managing pages, targeted ad campaigns, growing audiences.
  • Video & Audio: Editing short reels, YouTube videos, motion graphics, voiceovers.
  • Writing & Translation: Blog writing, copywriting, product descriptions, regional translations.
  • Website & Tech Help: Basic e-commerce setups (Shopify, WordPress), technical assist.

Teaching, Mentoring & Wellness

Share expertise or offer health guidance through live video calls.

  • School Tuitions: Subject coaching for students across all grade levels.
  • Skill Classes: 1-on-1 or group classes on niche subjects (stock market, coding, art).
  • Language Coaching: Conversational English or regional language lessons.
  • Fitness & Yoga: Live morning yoga, workout sessions, mindfulness coaching.
  • Diet & Nutrition: Personalized meal plans, calorie tracking, nutrition coaching.

Tailoring & Fashion Services

Monetize sewing, dress design, and artisan handwork from home.

  • Stitching Orders: Custom garment stitching with door-to-door fabric pickup/delivery.
  • Hand Embroidery: Artisan handwork (Aari, Zardosi, Maggam) on client fabrics.
  • Fashion Consultations: Live video calls to advise on fabrics, necklines, styling.

Home Foods & Shops

Sell homemade products or run a digital storefront.

  • Homemade Foods: Pickles, spice masalas, papads, festive sweets, healthy meals.
  • Handmade Crafts: Jewelry (terracotta, resin), candles, paintings, gift hampers.
  • Reselling (Share & Earn): Curating products and earning commissions via WhatsApp.
  • Digital Storefronts: Setting up an online shop to sell physical goods.

Voice Companionship

A safe, anonymous space offering friendly, empathetic conversation.

  • Empathetic Listening: Paid, non-judgmental phone conversations.
  • Protected Anonymity: In-app phone masking ensures complete privacy.

Astrology & Spiritual

Traditional guidance through private voice or video calls.

  • Astrology & Horoscope: Personalized readings and birth chart analysis.
  • Tarot & Numerology: Private sessions for personal or career guidance.
  • Vastu Consultations: Space planning to improve energy flow in homes/offices.

Platform Features & Safety

Core app capabilities designed to boost engagement, ensure security, and protect users.

Shoppable Reels & Feed

Short video reels and portfolio galleries to showcase craft process or products with direct "tap-to-buy" links.

Guaranteed Escrow

Upfront buyer payments held securely in escrow and released directly upon verified job delivery to prevent fraud.

In-App Masked Chat

Secure messaging with automatic contact masking to prevent transaction leaks and keep phone numbers hidden.

Integrated AI Safety

Background acoustic and sensor monitoring that calls SOS and broadcasts GPS if a user screams or throws their phone.
